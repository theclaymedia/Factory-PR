About Factory

The strategic discipline of a consultancy. The instinct of a newsroom. The relationships that come from living inside the industries we serve.

Factory is a communications agency with deep insight and an expansive network across technology, wellness, beauty, fashion, lifestyle, and consumer industries.

We know where people spend their time, what fills their feeds, what earns their attention, and what drives conversation.

Factory is of the moment, but also plays a long game. We think beyond launches and campaign cycles, helping brands build relevance, credibility, and momentum over time.

From founder-led startups to global brands, we work with companies at every stage of growth.
