About Factory

Factory is a communications agency with deep insight and an expansive network across technology, wellness, beauty, fashion, lifestyle, and consumer industries.

We bring together public relations, creator partnerships, social strategy, events, affiliate, executive communications, and brand positioning to help brands navigate change, launch new ideas, and reach new audiences.

We know where people spend their time, what fills their feeds, what earns their attention, and what drives conversation. Factory is of the moment, but also plays a long game, helping brands build relevance, credibility, and momentum over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Factory PR do?

Factory is a communications agency. We bring together public relations, creator partnerships, social strategy, events, affiliate, executive communications, and brand positioning to help brands navigate change, launch new ideas, and reach new audiences.

Which industries does Factory PR work with?

We work across fashion and luxury, beauty and lifestyle, technology, hospitality, and health and wellness, with companies at every stage of growth, from founder-led startups to global brands.

Where is Factory PR located?

Our global headquarters is at 920 Broadway, 12th Floor, in New York’s Flatiron District. Our Los Angeles office is at 8201 Beverly Blvd, Ste 302, and we operate in Miami near the Design District and Wynwood.

How do you choose the right influencers or VIP talent for a brand?

Our team selects talent based on brand alignment, audience relevance, engagement quality, and campaign goals. We analyze data, track previous brand collaborations, and consider tone, platform presence, and authenticity to ensure every influencer or VIP is a strategic fit that supports brand growth and cultural impact.

Can you manage both short-term campaigns and long-term partnerships?

Yes. We design and manage both one-off activations and multi-phase programs. Whether it is a time-sensitive product launch or a year-long ambassador relationship, we tailor the strategy, manage contracts and deliverables, and provide performance reporting throughout the partnership.

Do you handle gifting and celebrity product seeding in-house?

Absolutely. Our agency manages the entire gifting process internally. This includes talent list curation, branded packaging, outreach tracking, and securing media placements or organic content.

Can you support Award Season and Art Basel programs?

Yes. Our Los Angeles team offers specialized support for Award Season events, including suite activations, red carpet management, and talent dressing. In Miami, we specialize in Art Week strategy, helping brands cut through the noise with curated events, gallery partnerships, and collector engagement.
